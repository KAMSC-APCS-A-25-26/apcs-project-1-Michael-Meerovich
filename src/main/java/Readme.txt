You are a lost soul traveling through the world in your journey to reach the beyond.
You wake up in a forest, with a desire to reach a certain location, and you try to go on a journey to reach it.
In your travels you encounter a person who you can either save or rob. If you rob them you will gain more resources
throughout the story, however, if you help them, they will join you in your battles and attack enemies with you.
The person has information on where you need to go, from there you learn that you need to cross the mountains, which
you can do with 2 different methods. On the other side of the mountains you find the place that you were looking for,
however you have to fight a gatekeeper in order to enter.
